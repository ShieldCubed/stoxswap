How the Stoxswap desk works

1. Quote on our site

Pair, amount, destination and refund stay on stoxswap.com.

2. You pay our deposit

Send the exact quoted amount to the treasury address on the receipt.

3. We settle

Ops converts via OTC, CEX, or inventory. This is custodial during settlement, not an atomic swap.

4. You get paid

Payout goes to the destination address you entered.
